# Seraphin landing concept laboratory

This branch is an isolated sales and design exploration. It does not replace or deploy over the production Seraphin website.

## 01 — Living Lotus

**Hook:** Seraphin owns a memorable digital object: its flower mark becomes a living, tactile welcome rather than a static logo.

**Motion system:** a real-time WebGL lotus slowly breathes, follows pointer movement, and visibly opens when the visitor taps the primary call to action. Concentric brass halos and a slow photographic dolly reinforce the transformation.

**Best at:** brand recognition, premium restraint, differentiating Seraphin from template-based spa sites.

**Risk:** the metaphor carries more of the sale than the treatment catalogue, so the next section must quickly introduce services and proof.

## 02 — The Threshold

**Hook:** Sell the emotional crossing from Yerevan's noise into a quiet private room. The visitor does not browse a spa; they enter it.

**Motion system:** animated 3D portal frames travel through depth, luminous floor particles pull the eye forward, and sheer curtains physically open when the visitor chooses “Enter the quiet.”

**Best at:** immediate pitch-room impact, cinematic storytelling, making the physical venue feel aspirational.

**Risk:** more theatrical than informational. Booking and trust signals need to appear immediately below the fold.

## 03 — Ritual Oracle

**Hook:** Replace a generic service menu with a body-first question. One tap gives the visitor a real treatment, duration, and current price.

**Motion system:** a particle-built body breathes in real time while tactile brass forms orbit it; animated pulse rings make the interface feel responsive and sensorial.

**Best at:** conversion, mobile interaction, demonstrating useful personalization rather than decorative animation.

**Risk:** slightly less timeless than Living Lotus and needs careful language so it never implies medical diagnosis.

## Recommendation

- **Best contract-closing presentation:** The Threshold.
- **Best long-term Seraphin brand signature:** Living Lotus.
- **Best business and booking mechanic:** Ritual Oracle.
- **Strongest final direction:** use Living Lotus as the visual brand world, then place Ritual Oracle directly beneath it. Reserve The Threshold's curtain transition for campaign launches or a desktop-only opening moment.
